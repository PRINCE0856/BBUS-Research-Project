# READ THIS BEFORE USING ANY CITATION IN THIS FOLDER

Every source in these evidence files was found by web SEARCH only.

The network policy in the session that produced them blocked direct page and
PDF retrieval (403 at the egress gateway) for essentially every scholarly and
institutional domain: arxiv.org, worldbank.org, nber.org, tandfonline.com,
sciencedirect.com, wri-india.org, ideasforindia.in, voxdev.org and others.

That means: no paper below was read in full text. Author lists, years, page
numbers, DOIs and above all the NUMBERS come from search-engine snippets.

Consequences you must act on:

1. Open every link before citing it. Confirm the paper exists, that the
   authors and year are right, and that the figure you want to quote is
   actually in the paper and says what the snippet claimed.
2. Two specific discrepancies are already known and flagged in the files:
   the Delhi Metro harassment reduction appears as both 29% and 32%, and a
   claimed "transport reduces dropout by 15-25%" traces only to a
   non-academic aggregator with no primary source. Do not use the latter.
3. Several entries are explicitly marked as grey literature, small-sample or
   unverified. Those labels are load-bearing. Keep them.
4. CEEW's standard is that no citation enters an output unless a person has
   retrieved and read it. Nothing here meets that standard yet. These files
   are a search map, not a bibliography.
